Good to know.

Do I need to install .NET?+

No. Both the installer and portable ZIP include the runtime. You just need Windows 11 x64. For the portable version, extract the entire ZIP before running PaneShift.App.exe.

Does it work with administrator windows?+

PaneShift normally runs with standard privileges. To manage elevated windows, choose “Restart as administrator” from the tray or General settings and accept the Windows UAC prompt.

Where are my settings stored?+

Your settings stay on your PC, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\PaneShift\settings.json. The installer and portable version share them. Uninstalling preserves this file; start at login needs a fresh opt-in after reinstalling.

Can I contribute?+

Absolutely. Explore the source on GitHub, report an issue, suggest an improvement, or contribute code. PaneShift is an independent, MIT-licensed open-source project.
